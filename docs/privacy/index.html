Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

What we collect

If you reserve an order we collect your email address and the product and quantity you chose. We also record anonymous page activity (pages viewed, items added to cart) and the ad or link that brought you here.

How we use it

We use your email only to tell you about SWELTER: when your reserved order is ready and your launch discount. We use page activity to decide which products to make. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel to measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram. You can control ad personalisation in your Meta account settings.

Your choices

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. Reply to any of our emails to ask us to delete your details.

Payments

We have not taken payment for any order. Nothing is charged until a product ships and you confirm.
